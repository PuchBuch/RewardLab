Privacy

The full rules are on the How it works page.

Virtual points

This is a portfolio demo. Points are virtual, have no monetary value and cannot be bought, sold, exchanged or withdrawn. There are no deposits, wallets or real-world prizes: contest prizes are points too.

Data we store

Username, a hashed password (never the password itself), points, activity history and notifications. To limit fake accounts we also store a one-way hash of the IP address used at sign-up: the address itself is not stored and cannot be recovered from the hash. No e-mail address, no real name, no tracking or advertising.

Delete your data

You can delete your account and all its data at any time from the dashboard.
