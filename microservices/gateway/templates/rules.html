💎 Virtual points

RewardLab is a demo of reward mechanics. Points are virtual: they have no monetary value and cannot be bought, sold, exchanged, transferred or withdrawn. There are no deposits and no real-world prizes.

🧠 Daily quiz

  • One German vocabulary question per day (UTC), one attempt.
  • A correct answer gives +{{ quiz }} points.

🃏 Blackjack

A friendly card game against the dealer, played with your virtual points. Points cannot be bought or withdrawn, so you can never win or lose real money. Play for fun and take breaks.

  • Pick a bet: {% for c in chips %}{{ c }}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} points. The bet is taken when the cards are dealt.
  • Goal: get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Number cards count as their value, J/Q/K as 10, Ace as 1 or 11.
  • Hit takes a card, Stand ends your turn, Double (only on your first two cards) doubles the bet and takes exactly one more card.
  • The dealer draws to 16 and stands on all 17s.
  • Blackjack (Ace + 10-value card) pays 3 to 2. A regular win pays 1 to 1. A tie (push) returns your bet.
  • Cards come from a freshly shuffled {{ decks }}-deck shoe every hand. Everything is decided on the server.
  • Blackjack winnings do not count toward referral shares or the weekly contest.

🤝 Referral program

Share your personal invite link from the Friends page. A friend who signs up with it becomes your referral.

  • Every time your friend claims a bonus or answers the quiz correctly, you get a share of their reward on top. Their reward is never reduced.
  • After their {{ claims_needed }}rd hourly claim the friend becomes active: you get +{{ bonus_inviter }}, the friend gets a +{{ bonus_friend }} welcome bonus. This happens once per friend.

🏆 Weekly invite contest

  • The week runs from Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 UTC.
  • You score one point for each invited friend who becomes active during the week. Friends who only sign up do not count.
  • Prizes: {% for p in prizes %}#{{ loop.index }} +{{ p }}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} points.
  • On a tie, the member who reached the score first ranks higher.
  • Prizes are credited automatically at the start of the next week, and winners get a notification.
  • Live standings: Leaderboard → Weekly contest.

🔔 Notifications

You get a notification on your dashboard when a friend joins with your link, when a friend becomes active, when you reach a new referral level and when you win a prize in the weekly contest. The badge next to Dashboard shows how many are unread.

⚖️ Fair play rules

  • One account per person. Creating extra accounts to invite yourself is not allowed.
  • An invite from the same network (IP address) as the inviter does not count: the account is created, but without a referral.
  • At most {{ per_net }} new accounts can be created from one network in 24 hours.
  • No bots, scripts or any automation to claim bonuses, answer the quiz, play blackjack or create accounts.
  • Do not spam your invite link or post it where it is unwelcome.
  • No offensive, misleading or impersonating usernames.
  • Points cannot be traded. Any offers to buy or sell points or accounts are against the rules.

Breaking these rules may lead to points being reset, referral bonuses being removed or the account being deleted.

🙋 Your responsibilities

  • Keep your password safe. No e-mail is collected, so a lost password cannot be recovered.
  • Use only your own account and do not share it.
  • Follow the fair play rules above.
  • Invite only people who actually want to join.
  • Report bugs instead of exploiting them.

🛡 Our responsibilities

  • Store only the minimum data needed. See the privacy notice.
  • Never ask you for money, payment details or personal documents.
  • Let you delete your account and all its data at any time from the dashboard.
  • Keep this page up to date: the numbers shown here are always the current settings. Rules and rewards may change, as this is a demo.
